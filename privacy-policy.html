Privacy Policy

Updated: October 2, 2026

Who we are

InveStory is a personal finance tracker created by an independent developer ("we"). Contact: mnacik1988@gmail.com.

Sign-in with Google

To use the app you sign in with your Google account. From Google we receive your account identifier and email address. They are used to keep you signed in and to apply fair-use limits to the AI assistant. We never receive your Google password.

Your portfolio data

Everything you enter — deposits, coins, stocks, loans, notes and other values — is stored only on your device. We do not upload your portfolio to our servers, except the summary described in "AI assistant" when you use it.

Exchange connections

You can connect a crypto exchange (currently Bybit and Binance) with a read-only API key. The key and secret are stored only on your device and are never included in backups. The app refuses keys that allow trading, transfers or withdrawals. Bybit requests go directly from your device to Bybit. Binance does not accept such requests from a browser, so they pass through our server: it receives the public API key and an already signed request, forwards it to Binance and returns the answer. The secret key never leaves your device, and the server does not store or log keys or balances. Balances are stored only on your device. Signing out or disconnecting the exchange deletes the keys from the device.

Prices and exchange rates

To show current prices and rates, the app requests public market data from Binance, Bybit, CoinGecko, Finnhub, Yahoo Finance, the National Bank of Ukraine and other exchange-rate sources — directly or through our server. These requests contain only tickers, coin symbols or currency codes; the services also see your IP address.

AI assistant

The AI chat is optional. When you send a message, your message and a summary of your portfolio (totals by category, deposit terms and names, positions in stocks, bonds and crypto including coins from connected exchanges, exchange rates) are sent through our server to Anthropic, the provider of the Claude model, to generate a reply. We do not store the content of your conversations. To enforce limits we keep a daily request counter linked to your Google account identifier (deleted automatically after 2 days) and total token usage without any identifier (kept up to 90 days). If you do not use the AI chat, no portfolio data is sent.

Servers and hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, and our server runs on Cloudflare Workers. These providers may keep technical data such as IP address, device type and request time in their logs for a short period for security and operation. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, do not show advertising and do not use analytics or tracking tools.

Export and import

Export creates a backup file on your device. If you upload or send it to a cloud service or messenger, that service handles the file under its own rules.

Purchases

The app is currently free. If paid features are added, payments will be processed by Google Play or the App Store, and we will not receive your card details. This policy will be updated before that.

Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you delete it — in the app settings, by clearing the app data or by uninstalling the app. Signing out removes sign-in data and exchange keys from the device. Data on our server is deleted automatically within the periods stated above. You can ask us to delete any data related to you by email.

Your rights

Depending on your country (for example under the GDPR), you have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to object to its processing. Write to mnacik1988@gmail.com — we reply within 30 days.

International transfers

Cloudflare, Anthropic, Google and other providers may process data outside your country, including in the USA, under their own safeguards.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children.

Important

InveStory is not a financial advisor. Calculations, rates and forecasts are informational and may differ from actual bank or market values.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version; significant changes will be announced in the app.
