Privacy Policy

Who we are

InveStory is a personal finance tracker created by an independent developer ("we"). Contact: mnacik1988@gmail.com.

Sign-in with Google

To use the app you sign in with your Google account. From Google we receive your account identifier and email address. They are used to keep you signed in and to apply fair-use limits to the AI assistant. We never receive your Google password.

Your account on our server

When you sign in, we create an account record on our server: your Google account identifier, email address, dates of your first and latest visit, the number of AI requests, your access status (trial, subscription), settings made by the administrator (for example, a personal limit or a block) and the platform and device model you sign in from (for example, “Android 14 · SM-S918B”). It is needed to provide access, apply limits and prevent abuse. The record is kept while you use the app and is deleted when you delete your account (Settings → Delete account) or at your request.

Your portfolio data

Everything you enter — deposits, coins, stocks, loans, notes and other values — is stored on your device. We upload it to our server only if you turn on the optional cloud backup (see “Cloud backup (optional)” below), and we send the summary described in “AI assistant” when you use it.

Cloud backup (optional)

If you turn on cloud backup, a copy of your portfolio data — the same content as a file export: deposits, coins, stocks, loans, notes, chart history and app settings — is uploaded to our server and linked to your Google account identifier, so you can restore it after changing or losing your phone. The copy is encrypted on our server. The encryption key is held by the service operator, who can technically decrypt the copy; we do not read it, sell it or share it, and use it only to restore your data for you. Exchange API keys, bank tokens and purchase information are never included — they stay only on your device. We keep up to 3 latest copies; each records the date, the app version and the type of device it was made on. The backup is off until you agree to it; you can turn it off and delete all copies at any time in Settings, and the copies are deleted automatically when you delete your account.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes from our server your account record, all cloud backups and administrator-log entries that refer to you. To prevent abuse (so that deleting and re-creating an account does not lift a block or reset limits), we keep only your Google account identifier, whether the account was blocked, and the dates of your first sign-in and of the deletion — no email or other data; the daily AI request counter is not reset either and is deleted automatically within 7 days. Data stored on your device is erased only if you choose that option. You can also request deletion by email at mnacik1988@gmail.com or on the web page https://mnacik1988.github.io/kapital01/delete-account.html. Our infrastructure provider may keep technical database backups for a limited period (up to 30 days) before they are overwritten. Deleting your account does not cancel subscriptions bought through Google Play or the App Store — cancel those in the store.

Exchange connections

You can connect a crypto exchange (currently Bybit and Binance) with a read-only API key. The key and secret are stored only on your device and are never included in backups. The app refuses keys that allow trading, transfers or withdrawals. Bybit requests go directly from your device to Bybit. Binance does not accept such requests from a browser, so they pass through our server: it receives the public API key and an already signed request, forwards it to Binance and returns the answer. The secret key never leaves your device, and the server does not store or log keys or balances. Balances are stored only on your device. Signing out or disconnecting the exchange deletes the keys from the device.

Prices and exchange rates

To show current prices and rates, the app requests public market data from Binance, Bybit, CoinGecko, Finnhub, Yahoo Finance, the National Bank of Ukraine and other exchange-rate sources — directly or through our server. These requests contain only tickers, coin symbols or currency codes; the services also see your IP address. Coin and company logos are loaded through our server from jsDelivr, CoinGecko and Finnhub — they receive only the ticker, not your IP address.

AI assistant

The AI chat is optional. When you send a message, your message and a summary of your portfolio (totals by category, deposit terms and names, positions in stocks, bonds and crypto including coins from connected exchanges, exchange rates) are sent through our server to Anthropic, the provider of the Claude model, to generate a reply. We do not store the content of your conversations. To enforce limits we keep a daily request counter linked to your Google account identifier (deleted automatically within 7 days) and total token usage without any identifier (kept up to 90 days). If you do not use the AI chat, no portfolio data is sent.

Servers and hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, and our server runs on Cloudflare Workers. These providers may keep technical data such as IP address, device type and request time in their logs for a short period for security and operation. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, do not show advertising and do not use analytics or tracking tools.

Export and import

Export creates a backup file on your device. If you upload or send it to a cloud service or messenger, that service handles the file under its own rules.

Purchases

The app is currently free. If paid features are added, payments will be processed by Google Play or the App Store, and we will not receive your card details. This policy will be updated before that.

Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you delete it — in the app settings, by clearing the app data or by uninstalling the app. Signing out removes sign-in data and exchange keys from the device. Daily counters on our server are deleted automatically within the periods stated above; your account record is kept while you use the app. You can delete your account and all data on our server yourself in the app (Settings → Delete account), or ask us to delete any data related to you by email. Cloud backups, if you turned them on, are kept until you delete them in Settings or delete your account.

Your rights

Depending on your country (for example under the GDPR), you have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to object to its processing. Write to mnacik1988@gmail.com — we reply within 30 days.

International transfers

Cloudflare, Anthropic, Google and other providers may process data outside your country, including in the USA, under their own safeguards.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children.

Important

InveStory is not a financial advisor. Calculations, rates and forecasts are informational and may differ from actual bank or market values.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version; significant changes will be announced in the app.

Секретный ключ не покидает ваше устройство, а сервер не хранит и не записывает ни ключи, ни балансы. Балансы хранятся только на устройстве. Выход из аккаунта или отключение биржи удаляет ключи с устройства.

Цены и курсы

Чтобы показывать текущие цены и курсы, приложение запрашивает публичные рыночные данные у Binance, Bybit, CoinGecko, Finnhub, Yahoo Finance, Национального банка Украины и других источников курсов — напрямую или через наш сервер. Такие запросы содержат только тикеры, символы монет или коды валют; сервисы также видят ваш IP-адрес. Логотипы монет и компаний загружаются через наш сервер из jsDelivr, CoinGecko и Finnhub — они получают только тикер, без вашего IP-адреса.
